# Highlights

*Standing liquidity backstops in dealer intermediated foreign exchange markets*

Elsevier requires 3 to 5 bullets, each at most 85 characters including spaces,
submitted as a separate editable file. At submission this is converted to
`Highlights.docx`, since Elsevier does not accept PDF for this item.

- Dealer withdrawal under stress is modelled endogenously in an agent based FX market.
- A facility that stays open reduces the amplitude of the crisis dislocation.
- Controlling for amplitude, the speed of spread recovery is left unchanged.
- Migrating flow is the flow dealers shed, so facility losses fall in stress.
- The facility never pays for itself, so a measurable subsidy sustains it.
